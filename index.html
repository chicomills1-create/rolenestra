THE DETAILS

A few things worth knowing.

Will RoleNestra apply to jobs for me?

No. The launch concept focuses on drafting career materials. Applications remain under the job seeker’s direct control.

Does this guarantee interviews or ATS success?

No. Hiring outcomes and automated screening systems vary. No ranking, interview or employment result is guaranteed.

Can it invent experience to improve my chances?

No. Every education, experience and certification claim must be supported by information you provide and approve.

Should I enter personal information in the demo?

No. The demo uses fictional sample resumes. Do not enter personal, sensitive or confidential information into a public preview.
